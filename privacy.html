Privacy policy

Updated November 19, 2026

The short version

HitchCal keeps your information on your phone. There's no account, and we don't collect, sell or share your data.

What HitchCal stores

Your schedule, pay rates, notes, certifications, followed crew schedules, photos you add and your settings. They're saved in your browser's storage on this device only. Clearing your browser data, or using Erase all data in Settings, removes them.

What leaves your device

  • Links you share. A family or crew link carries the schedule inside the link itself. Anyone you send it to can see that schedule. We don't store it.
  • Files you export. Backups, calendar files and PDFs go wherever you save or send them.
  • Fonts. The app loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so Google receives your device's network address when the app loads.
  • Feedback you send. If you email us, we receive your message and any diagnostics you choose to include: app version, device type, settings and recent errors.

Analytics and crash reports

HitchCal doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking today. If we add privacy-friendly usage counts or crash reports, we'll update this policy first and say exactly what they collect.

Children

HitchCal isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and the app mentions it in What's new.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use Send feedback in the app's Settings.
