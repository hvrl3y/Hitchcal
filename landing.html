No app store needed

On your home screen in ten seconds.

HitchCal is a web app. Add it once and it opens full screen like any other app, even without signal.

iPhone

  1. Open HitchCal in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open HitchCal in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is it free?

Yes. Everything you see here is free to use.

Where is my information kept?

On your phone only. There's no account and nothing is uploaded. You can back it up to a file any time.

Does it work for my rotation?

Any even or uneven rotation works: 14/14, 21/7, 28/28, 7/7 days and nights, and more. Set your start date and shift times once.

What if my hitch changes?

Tap the day, then change the hitch: held over, released early, started late, sick days and call-ins. Everything recalculates.
